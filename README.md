## Projects

**[barrier-option-mc-pricer](https://github.com/LouisBennetto/barrier-option-mc-pricer)**
Prices a down-and-out barrier option by five independent routes and makes them
agree: four Monte Carlo estimators reaching 13.9x variance reduction, a
Crank-Nicolson PDE solver, and the Rubinstein-Reiner closed form, reconciled to
within 1.06 standard errors once the discrete-monitoring correction is applied.

**[avellaneda-stoikov-market-maker](https://github.com/LouisBennetto/avellaneda-stoikov-market-maker)**
Optimal market making under inventory risk, showing that quote skew rather than
spread width is what actually controls inventory, with a 78% reduction in
inventory variance and an exact three-term PnL decomposition that holds to
machine precision.

**[Reward-Function-Design-in-Discrete-Domain-Reinforcement-Learning](https://github.com/LouisBennetto/Reward-Function-Design-in-Discrete-Domain-Reinforcement-Learning)**
BSc dissertation comparing seven reward-design methods in a discrete stochastic
game, finding that inverse RL and potential-based shaping win under different
learning algorithms, with an 11 point win-rate gain over the sparse baseline.
